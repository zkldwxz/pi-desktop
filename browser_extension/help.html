Connect your browser to pi-desktop

Open pi-desktop, go to Settings → Browser, and enable browser access. Restart this extension after opening the app if it still shows “not running”.

Click the extension button to share a tab. PI can use the tabs in the pi-desktop group. BaoCode uses its own separate extension and tab group.

连接到 pi-desktop

打开 pi-desktop，在“设置 → 浏览器”中启用浏览器访问。如果仍提示未运行，请在打开应用后重新加载此扩展。

点击扩展按钮即可共享当前标签页。PI 使用 pi-desktop 标签组；BaoCode 的扩展和标签组可以同时存在。
